Privacy Policy – Mak Player

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Mak Player ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Mak Player mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Mak Player does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to use its music playback features. The app does not operate its own servers or collect personal music libraries.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The following information is stored locally on your device:

  • Music playback preferences.
  • Favorites and playlists.
  • Recently played tracks and listening history.
  • App settings and preferences.

This information is not uploaded to our servers.

3. Advertising – Google AdMob

Mak Player uses Google AdMob to display banner and interstitial advertisements. Google AdMob may collect or process certain information, including:

  • Advertising identifiers.
  • Device information.
  • IP address and approximate location.
  • Advertising interactions.
  • Diagnostic information.

This information may be used to provide, measure, and improve advertising services, including personalized advertising where permitted.

For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy:
Google Privacy Policy

4. Internet Connection

Mak Player uses an internet connection to load and display advertisements. The core music playback functionality works locally on your device.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or directly share users' personal information. However, Google AdMob may process information according to its own privacy policies and applicable settings.

6. Data Security

Your music library, playlists, favorites, and playback history are stored locally on your device. We do not maintain a central database containing this information.

7. Children's Privacy

Mak Player is not specifically designed for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
